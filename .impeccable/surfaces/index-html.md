# Surface brief: index.html (Auyama Onepager)

Scope: the whole onepage site. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: lunch guests from Freising, Altstadt walkers, international students; mostly on a phone, deciding where to eat now. Second reader: the owner, seeing a finished pitch.
Action: know if it is open, call or get directions, browse the monthly menu.
Proof/content: real monthly menu with prices, live opening status, the confirmed film night, real photos (pitch placeholders), Instagram.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, JSON content files, logo unaltered as round badge, pinned palette (pumpkin, deep green, cream; accents mango, tropical green, terracotta), no draft or placeholder labels, no invented dates/claims, no dashes in running copy.
Memorable moment: the page is a Venezuelan tile floor; the dish ABC tiles flip.
Unresolved: real photos from the owner; salsa night dates.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Venezuelan cement tile floor (baldosa hidráulica) seen from above, the floor under the table in a Caracas casa. It refuses the cream-paper restaurant template with a split photo-and-copy hero and equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pigments of poured cement tiles: deep verde field (#14321D, grout #0B220E), auyama pumpkin (#D08230), mango (#F2B53A), cal white (#F6EFE2), terracotta (#B04A2A) and tropical green (#2E7D4F) as rare accents. Square tiles with 6px grout gaps, faintly rounded corners, authored SVG tile ornaments (corner quarter circles, four-petal center) and cenefa border bands between rooms. Gloock for display, Bricolage Grotesque for text.

STORY: Venezuela on a plate, in the middle of Freising, open now. The visitor sees the food, the live status and the call and menu actions, browses the monthly menu, learns unfamiliar dishes from the tile ABC, adds an event to the calendar, finds the hours and the route.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full bleed green tile floor, laid as a 6 by 4 tile grid on desktop. Headline in Gloock at display scale over the left three columns with one line of copy and the Anrufen (pumpkin) and Route actions; the overhead bowl photo fills a 3 by 3 tile block on the right; a bottom row of working tiles: live status (cal), Monatskarte link (pumpkin), ¡Bienvenidos! (mango), ornament tiles between. Mobile: two column tiles, headline and actions first, status and Monatskarte tiles, then the photo block.

FORM: Mosaico, cement tile floor; position 3 of my ordered list of 7; seed key 2a34c661 (degraded roll, no challengers).

Signature interaction: tiles settle into the floor on load (one orchestrated entrance), and the ABC tiles flip to explain each dish; menu terms open the same explanation in place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
