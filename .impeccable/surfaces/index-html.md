# Surface brief: index.html and speisekarte.html (Auyama)

Scope: the onepage site and the full monthly menu page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: lunch guests from Freising, Altstadt walkers, international students; mostly on a phone, deciding where to eat now. Second reader: the owner, seeing a finished pitch.
Action: know if it is open, call or get directions, get hungry from the menu preview, open the full menu.
Proof/content: real monthly menu with prices, live opening status, real dated events only, photos (to be replaced by the owner's own), Instagram.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, JSON content files, logo unaltered, pinned palette, no draft or placeholder labels, no invented dates/claims, no dashes in running copy, du-form.
Memorable moment: the tile floor hero; the order slip that picks today's dish.
Unresolved: owner's own photos; real salsa night dates; owner's name for the imprint.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Venezuelan cement tile floor (baldosa hidráulica) seen from above, with the playful things of a Latin American table placed on it: prints, stamps, stickers, price tags, an order slip. It refuses the cream-paper restaurant template and the equal-card menu grid.

OWN-WORLD: Pigments of poured cement tiles: verde field (#14321D, grout #0B220E), auyama (#D08230), mango (#F2B53A), cal (#F6EFE2), terracota deep (#9A3C24), tropico (#2A7448). Square tiles with 6px corners and 8px grout, authored flor ornaments, cenefa bands, a scroll-linked auyama ribbon, cal-framed photo prints tilted 3 to 5 degrees, mango stickers and price tags. Gloock for display, Bricolage Grotesque for text.

STORY: Venezuela on a plate in Freising, open now. The visitor meets the house (¡Hola, Freising!), gets hungry from the dish preview, lets the order slip pick a dish by mood, opens the full menu, adds an event to the calendar, finds hours and the route.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full bleed tile floor filling the viewport: 6 by 4 tiles on desktop and landscape tablets, 4 columns filling the screen on portrait tablets, 2 columns on phones. Headline slab with Anrufen and Route, bowl photo block, live status tile, Monatskarte tile, ¡Bienvenidos! tile, ornament tile.

FORM: Mosaico, cement tile floor; position 3 of my ordered list of 7; seed key 2a34c661 (degraded roll, no challengers). Extended on user request with more playfulness.

Signature interaction: tiles settle into the floor on load; the order slip ("comanda") slides in with a dish picked from the monthly menu by mood.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
